Our story

我们的故事

From a kopitiam table to forever

2019

Petaling Street, Kuala Lumpur

A rainy night, one empty seat at a kopitiam table and a shared bowl of tong sui. We talked until they stacked the chairs.

2021

George Town, Penang

Our first trip together. Too much char kway teow, a borrowed bicycle and getting happily lost among the shophouses.

2023

Melaka River

He planned to propose on this bridge. He lost his nerve and bought her cendol instead.

2025

Cameron Highlands

Sunrise over the tea fields, one knee in the wet grass and the easiest yes.

2026

Langkawi

One last sunset before the big day. Next stop: a rooftop in KL, at midnight.
